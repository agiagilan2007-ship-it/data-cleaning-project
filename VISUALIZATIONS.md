# Visualizations and Findings

The notebook creates four charts from `cleaned_data.csv`: department counts, age distribution, age versus salary, and average salary by joining year. The SVG previews are committed for display in the README; running the notebook also creates matching PNG files.

## Current findings

- The cleaned dataset contains 29 employee records and 6 columns.
- Department counts are HR: 15, IT: 6, and Unknown/other: 8.
- Ages are concentrated in the 30–49 ranges (20 of 29 employees).
- The age/salary scatter plot shows variation across ages, so age alone does not explain salary in this sample.
- Average salary by joining year is an exploratory aggregate and should be interpreted alongside the number of employees in each year.

Charts are generated by `cleaningdata_final_submission_tested.ipynb` and saved under `visualizations/`.
